# Disclosure Boundary

## Approved public material

- anonymized problem statement;
- sanitized architecture;
- order/risk-management concepts;
- generic validation methodology;
- capability-level acceptance outcomes;
- engineering lessons and limitations;
- final public-safe version label.

## Excluded material

- client identity or handle;
- private repository or file-system paths;
- confidential source code;
- account identifiers, balances or statements;
- provider credentials or authentication details;
- private screenshots, videos or logs;
- private conversations;
- commercial/payment details;
- native backup/export files;
- proprietary strategy logic or signals;
- machine identifiers.

The public repository must remain independently authored and must never become a mirror of the private delivery repository.
